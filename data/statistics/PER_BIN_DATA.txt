81-CASE PARAMETER SCAN: PER-ENERGY-BIN COMPARISONS

We provide point_contributions.csv.gz as a losslessly compressed CSV table.
It contains the reference-selected bins used for the fixed-parameter chi-square
maps, not all bins of the original spectra. No private model source is included.

Coverage
--------
81 pairs of electron temperature and total slab optical depth (a 9 x 9 grid),
two models (jones and comppsc), both escape surfaces, two inclination windows
(0--45 and 45--90 degrees), and Stokes I and Q. The table has 146248 rows.
We use 5 eV blackbody seeds and Lambert-law lower-boundary injection. Each MCRT
case has 400 independent batches of one million injected photons.

Columns
-------
model: jones = recursion + Jones kernel; comppsc = modified compPSc, base mesh.
stokes: I or Q.
case: stable parameter-case identifier; use the numerical parameter columns.
kTe_keV: electron temperature in keV.
tau: total slab Thomson optical depth.
boundary: up or down (upper or lower escape surface).
window: th000_045 or th045_090; inclination is relative to the surface normal.
band_bins: number of energy bins whose centers fall within the requested band.
statistical_support_bins: number passing the reference statistical support cut.
E_left_keV, E_right_keV: measured energy-bin edges in keV.
model_density: model bin-averaged Stokes photon density.
MCRT_density: MCRT bin-averaged Stokes photon density, in our comparison convention.
MCRT_mean_SE: standard error of the MCRT mean, not the scatter of individual batches.
chi2_contribution: ((model_density - MCRT_density) / MCRT_mean_SE)^2.

All three density/error columns are per injected photon per keV per steradian.
They are photon densities, not E-weighted or E^2-weighted spectra and not detector
counts. Q is positive for polarization parallel to the slab surface. The raw
MCRT convention has already been reversed once; do not reverse this table again.
We conservatively average the model over the MCRT energy bins. We do not fit
any amplitude, and both models share exactly the same MCRT reference bins.

Selection and limitations
-------------------------
We select bin centers between 0.005 keV and 5 kTe, at least 50 escaped packets,
at least 8 occupied batches, and E_center^2 I exceeding 1e-8 of its reference
band peak. The Stokes density and its SE must be finite, with SE > 0.
There is no Q-significance cut. This table cannot recover excluded bins or
their packet counts; changing the support requires the original full spectra.

For each (model, case, boundary, window, stokes), we sum chi2_contribution and
divide by the number of retained rows. The resulting chi2/N reproduces the
bundled fixed-parameter summary. We use diagonal statistical errors only:
inter-bin covariance and model numerical errors are not included. In particular,
compPSc iteration convergence does not certify full numerical-mesh convergence.
The repeated MCRT values in the two model groups are not independent simulations.

Reading and reproducing the statistic
------------------------------------
import pandas as pd
p = pd.read_csv('data/statistics/point_contributions.csv.gz')
keys = ['model', 'case', 'boundary', 'window', 'stokes']
summary = p.groupby(keys).chi2_contribution.agg(['sum', 'count', 'mean'])
# sum = chi2; count = N; mean = chi2/N (zero fitted parameters).
