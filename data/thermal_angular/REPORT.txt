Thermally averaged angular Mueller matrix test (2026-10-26)
==========================================================

Scope
-----
kTe=20 keV, total Thomson tau=1 and 10, 5 eV blackbody photon-number
seeds, unpolarized Lambert lower-boundary injection. Both surfaces and
0-30, 30-60, 60-90 degree escape windows are integrated. Reference MCRT:
400 million histories per case, existing public caches. No new MCRT or fit.
Original/public notebooks and production model sources are unchanged.

Controlled change
-----------------
The existing Jones energy transition is retained byte-for-byte. Only the
angular/polarization operator is changed. Isotropic Maxwell-Juttner electrons
are integrated with generalized Laguerre gamma quadrature and angular Gaussian/
periodic quadrature. The electron-rest-frame Thomson Jones amplitude is boosted
with gauge-corrected polarization four-vectors. The photon-number differential
angular rate contains

  (1 - beta.n_in) / [gamma^2 (1 - beta.n_out)^2]

multiplying the rotated rest-frame Mueller matrix. This includes relative
collision flux and the outgoing-solid-angle aberration Jacobian. We integrate
the incident azimuth and retain all four axisymmetric I/Q matrix elements.
Q is positive parallel to the slab surface, as in the manuscript.

This is an energy-integrated, RECOIL-FREE thermal angular matrix. It is NOT
the full energy-dependent thermal Klein-Nishina redistribution matrix.
We still factor the scalar Jones energy kernel from angular/polarization
transport, and retain Thomson opacity. Thus this test isolates thermal angular
motion; energy-angle-polarization correlations and KN angular/opacity changes
remain absent. It must not be described as exact thermal Compton transport.

The two a,b functions no longer close the angular operator. We instead use
discrete ordinates in signed mu, segmented at the escape-window boundaries.
Cell-constant source propagation and cell-mean fields are integrated exactly
against the attenuation exponential. No output amplitude is fitted or rescaled.

Verification
------------
Displayed runs: tau1 ndepth=2048, tau10 ndepth=4096, 120 signed ordinates;
thermal kernel 801 scattering-cosine samples, 32 gamma x 32 electron-cosine
x 64 electron-azimuth nodes, 96 incident-azimuth nodes. Coarse runs independently
use half the depth cells, 72 ordinates and 401/24/24/48/64 kernel settings.
Cold limit is checked against the original two-component FFT recursion.
Angular photon closure errors are below 1e-12 for these refined cases; no
negative source I or violation of |Q|<=I was encountered.
The largest coarse/fine change, normalized by the MCRT E^2 spectrum peak,
is 5e-6 in I and 1.41e-4 in Q (six windows/surfaces, both tau cases).
Cold implementation versus original production spectra differs by at most
2e-6 (I) and 1.8e-5 (Q), using the same peak scaling.
Additional depth-only/angular-only checks are stored as separate n8192/a28 runs.
Doubling tau10 depth cells changes E^2 I/Q by 1.21e-6/1.63e-5 of their
respective global model peaks; the independent angle check is recorded in
independent_refinement.json.
These are experiment-level checks, not global validation across temperatures.

Comparison
----------
All models are integrated into the EXACT original MCRT energy bins before
residuals are formed. No log-flux interpolation or amplitude matching.
Use count>=50, positive finite SE, E^2 I_MCRT>1e-8 of its peak, and the stated
energy interval. Q is signed; no Q significance threshold is imposed.
chi2/N is a fixed-model diagnostic with diagonal MCRT errors, not a refitted
reduced chi-square or a formal model-selection probability. Aggregate entries
sum chi-square and N across the three non-overlapping angle windows.

Interpretation
--------------
Q improves strongly, particularly tau=1 where the broad negative trough and
positive seed-region feature approach MCRT/compPSc. The thermal angular
correction explains a large part, not all, of the cold-matrix Q discrepancy.
I is not uniformly improved: improvements in the 0.1-40 keV aggregate can
coexist with deterioration at individual angles or in the full band at tau=10.
The optically thick high-energy Q shape/sign discrepancy also remains.
The remaining mismatch motivates a joint energy-angle-polarization kernel;
these results do not establish which omitted effect alone causes it.
